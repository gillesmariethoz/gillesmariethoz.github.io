Recycling PET Bottles into 3D Printing Filament

01

The project

Homemade system to recycle PET bottles and turn them into filament usable for 3D printing.

02

Process

The bottle is first cut into one long continuous strip, which is then heated and rolled to form a hollow filament. Getting it right took a very large number of trials: the heating temperature directly affects the filament's consistency and strength, and many combinations had to be tested before finding a reliable, repeatable setting.

03

Result

After this testing phase, the system produces prints with very few defects, with recycled filament of good enough quality for clean prints.

04

Recognition

This project caught the interest of RPM (Recyclage Plastique Maison, home plastic recycling), for whom I was able to reproduce the system.
