01

The project

The goal was to develop a machine capable of cutting connector strips, for the client company DSMI electronics SA.

02

How it works

The machine is built around a pneumatic cylinder that moves back and forth: each cycle picks up a part, cuts it, then ejects it, for a target rate of around 1.5 parts per second.

03

Continuing the project

The diploma thesis, completed in 197 hours, was finished on time, but the machine could not be built at the time due to overly long delivery times for certain parts. I later had the chance to keep working on it, to see the project through to the end.

04

What I learned

This project taught me a lot, in particular that it isn't always necessary to optimize relentlessly for the lowest possible weight if it comes at the expense of the assembly's rigidity.
